Detection Layers

Our detection process consists of several stages, each designed to analyze different aspects of the video to determine its authenticity.

Layer 01
Lip Movement Analysis
Tracking facial landmarks to detect inconsistencies between lip motion and speech patterns.
Layer 02
Audio Analysis
Examining spectral features and voice patterns for signs of synthetic audio generation.
Layer 04
AI Verification Layer
A final neural pass combining all signals for a confidence-weighted authenticity score.
